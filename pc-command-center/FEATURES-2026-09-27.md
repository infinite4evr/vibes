# Feature expansion — 2026-09-27

This build completes the requested control-center feature set. Existing mature capabilities were retained and integrated rather than duplicated.

## Included

1. **System Health Engine** — health score, disk/RAM/swap/temperature/battery/update/service/security/time checks, actionable findings, health history and background alerts.
2. **Storage health** — SMART/NVMe JSON parsing, temperature, power-on hours, spare blocks, wear/percentage-used, media/data errors, drive/partition/USB management.
3. **Performance monitor** — live CPU/per-core, memory/swap, disk I/O, network throughput, temperature/GPU and pressure indicators; Processes supplies top consumers.
4. **Startup performance analyzer** — `systemd-analyze` time, blame and critical-chain support plus boot history and startup management.
5. **Network diagnostics** — gateway, internet, DNS and HTTPS layered diagnosis, Wi-Fi, interfaces, traffic, ports/process owners, connections, DNS, VPN/hotspot and speed test. A reusable core diagnostic and `pc network-check` are now available.
6. **Security Center** — firewall, exposed ports, SSH posture/hardening, failed/remote-login context, automatic/security updates, screen lock, autologin, remote desktop, Secure Boot and disk-encryption checks.
7. **Doctor 2.0** — `pc doctor`, dashboard health score and fixable checks use the same health model and safe Step actions.
8. **Preview/repair/rollback model** — state-changing actions are represented as Steps and shown before execution; backups/snapshots and reversible controls are exposed where the platform supports them.
9. **Task Centre** — running/recent work, output, cancellation, scheduled background alerts/checkups and Keep Awake status.
10. **Exportable diagnostic report** — HTML/text reports, health findings and system facts; share/privacy mode redacts names, addresses and identifiers. Support ZIP remains available.
11. **Command/search integration** — app command palette and GNOME search provider index pages/actions/settings with troubleshooting synonyms.
12. **Proactive notifications** — opt-in background health alerts and weekly checkups via user systemd timers.
13. **Optimization Advisor** — NEW: evidence-based, non-destructive recommendations with expected benefit, risk and a review/apply route. It explicitly avoids placebo RAM cleaners and blind service disabling. Available in Maintenance → Advisor and as `pc optimize`.

## Safety principle

Read-only probes degrade to “unavailable” wherever possible. Optimization advice never applies itself. Destructive or privileged actions continue through the existing preview/confirmation runner.
