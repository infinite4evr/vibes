# CHAPTER VA

## LAY-OFF AND RETRENCHMENT

#### 25C. **Right of workmen laid-off for compensation**

… he shall be **paid compensation** for all days of lay-off.

## STATE AMENDMENT

… for the words “all days”, substitute **“forty-five days”.**

#### 26. Penalty for illegal strikes

\(1) Any workman who commences an illegal strike shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to **one month.**
